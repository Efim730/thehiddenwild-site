Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

"The Hidden Wild Publisher" is a personal tool used only by the owner of the channel "The Hidden Wild" to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

What data is used

The tool uses an access token issued by TikTok or Meta after the owner signs in and gives permission. The token is stored only on the owner's own computer. The tool does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person.

What is sent

Only the videos, titles and captions the owner chooses to publish are sent to TikTok and Instagram.

Third parties

Data you provide to TikTok and Meta is handled according to their own privacy policies.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in the TikTok or Instagram account settings, which invalidates the stored token.
